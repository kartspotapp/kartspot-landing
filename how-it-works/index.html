How it works

Live pit strategy for endurance karting

Step by step

From live timing to a
working pit strategy.

01

Connect your timing

Create an event and point it at your live feed. KartSpot works with Apex Timing and RaceFacer and reads every lap for every kart automatically: no spreadsheets, no stopwatch, no typing. Assign your karts and drivers once, and you're set for the whole race.

02

Read every kart's true pace

Raw timing is noisy: traffic, mistakes and pit laps hide real speed. KartSpot shows best lap next to a clean average, built only from laps within a second of that kart's best, so you read genuine pace and not noise. Each kart earns a pace rating once it has a few flying laps to warm up (shown as ---- until rated).

03

Catch every pit stop, automatically

The instant a kart enters the pits, KartSpot flags it as ---- so a slow in-lap never fools you. It marks the out-lap (PIT OUT) for the kart's first laps back up to speed, and keeps the long combined pit lap out of your averages, so a stop never poisons a kart's pace numbers. Detection is driven by the pit signal itself, not a guess from lap time.

04

Plan your stints & driver rotation

Lay out your rotation on a shared strategy board: assign drivers to karts, follow each stint as it happens, and adjust on the fly. Everyone on the team sees the same board, updated live — no group chat, no confusion about who's next.

05

Put a board in your crew's hands

Share a link and your crew opens the mobile pit board on their phones, right on the pit wall, no login needed. They see the same live pace and pit picture you do, so a call from the stand reaches the wall instantly.

06

Never lose your race

Refresh the page, drop signal, switch device: your stints and data survive. KartSpot reconnects and picks up exactly where it left off, so you're never flying blind at the worst possible moment.

Questions

Good to know.

Which timing systems does it work with?

Apex Timing and RaceFacer — point KartSpot at your track's live timing and you're in.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs entirely in your browser — nothing to download or set up.

Does it work on phone and tablet?

Yes — desktop, tablet and phone. Your crew can run the board from a phone in the lane.

How does the fast / neutral / slow rating work?

KartSpot reads each kart's clean lap pace and rates it against the field in real time, so you see who's quick at a glance.

What does it cost?

Pay per race weekend, or monthly, or yearly — see Pricing above.

Is my race saved after it ends?

Yes. Every race is archived — fastest lap, most consistent team, and every team's stints and laps — to review any time.

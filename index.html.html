● WHAT'S INSIDE

One platform for the whole race.

From the timing feed to the pit board to the post-race report — KartSpot keeps your engineer, your crew and your drivers on the same data.

01

Live Timing

Connect an Apex or RaceFacer event by URL. KartSpot reads the live feed and rebuilds corrected standings, gaps and lap deltas in real time — columns mapped from the feed's own structure, so it works across tracks and languages.

ApexRaceFacerAuto outlap
02

Pit Strategy

Run the pit queue and pit board the way real crews do — FIFO row assignment, kart quality flags, took-from / returned history, and undo. The board syncs instantly between the engineer's screen and every crew phone in the lane.

Pit queueQuality flagsUndo
03

Driver & Kart Analytics

Every stint, swap and kart is saved. Compare avg and best before and after a change, track driver and kart trends across an event, and tell whether a slow kart was the kart — or the team that handed it over.

Stint historyAvg / BestTrust panel
04

Cloud Sync

PC, tablet and pit-crew phones stay in lockstep through the cloud. Share a live event with a link or a QR code — no installs, no setup on each device. Everyone sees the same board the moment it changes.

QR joinMulti-deviceRoles
● FROM URL TO PIT LANE

Live in four steps.

STEP 01

Create the event

Name your race and set the duration. Your team and drivers carry over from last time.

STEP 02

Paste the timing URL

Drop in the Apex or RaceFacer live link. KartSpot connects and maps the columns.

STEP 03

Bring the crew in

Share a link or QR code. Crew, engineer and viewers each join in their own role.

STEP 04

Run the race

Work the board live. Everything is saved for the report when you cross the line.
